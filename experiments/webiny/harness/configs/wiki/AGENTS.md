# Working in this repository

This repository has a wiki in `wiki/`. It is the team's shared knowledge: architecture, feature references, code-style rules, specs, past issues and how-to guides. The index is `wiki/README.md`.

## Before you change code

- Read `wiki/README.md` and then every wiki page relevant to the area you are working in. Do not rely on the code alone: the wiki explains why things are the way they are.
- Read the code-style rules in `wiki/ai-context/code-style/` before writing or editing code, and follow them.
- Check the specs and issues pages for anything related to your task.
- If you are unsure how a feature works, look it up in the wiki first.

## When you finish

- End your final message with a line that starts with `Wiki pages consulted:` followed by the pages you read.
